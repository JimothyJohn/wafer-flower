# Design Guide

This is a high-level design guide that will aid with the creation of an optimal sectional mounting frame based on specific design criteria.

## Fabrication

This will be manufactured on a Bambu H2S printer.

## Concepts

### Symmetry

All components should be idnetical and designed in such a way that they easily assemble together without tools. There will be an assembly jig that attaches to each frame component, and the sculpture will be mounted by taking advantage of the same jig mounting features to attach it to the wall.

### Footprint

The frame should be as low profile as possible while still limiting interference from 300mm wafers neighbored, mounted, and tilted towards its adjacent components.

### Drivetrain

Each segment will incorporate a spiral bevel rack on the inner (outer is possible in the future) radius that will engage a pinion to rotate the entire frame at a constant velocity.
