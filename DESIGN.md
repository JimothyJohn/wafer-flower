# Design Guide

This is a high-level design guide that will aid with the creation of an optimal sectional mounting frame based on specific design criteria.

## Fabrication

This will be manufactured on a Bambu H2S printer.

## Concepts

### Drivetrain

Each segment will incorporate a spiral bevel rack on the inner (outer is possible in the future) radius that will engage a pinion to rotate the entire frame at a constant velocity.
